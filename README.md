## 🖐️ How Gesture Control Works

GesturePortal uses MediaPipe to track hand and finger movement through your webcam. MediaPipe is the computer-vision component that helps the app understand where your hand is and how it is moving.

When GesturePortal recognizes a gesture, it uses that movement to control the webcam portal. Instead of reaching for a mouse or keyboard, you can keep your hands in view and guide the experience with gestures.

The gesture layer is designed to feel direct and visual. You see yourself through the webcam, move your hand, and the portal responds. This makes GesturePortal useful for creative exploration, live styling, and hands-free demonstrations.

## 🧰 Common Questions

### Do I need to know how to code?

No. GesturePortal is intended for everyday Windows users. You download the application from the official page and open it. The gesture control and AI styling run through the app interface.

### Do I need a special webcam?

A standard webcam is enough. Many Windows laptops have a built-in camera, and external USB webcams also work well. The important thing is that Windows can see the webcam and that the camera is not being used by another program.

### Is GesturePortal a cloud service?

GesturePortal is a local AI webcam portal. The gesture tracking and the ComfyUI-based styling workflow are designed to run on your own Windows computer.

### What do Portrait v2, FLUX, and Qwen do?

These are the styling components behind the full-scene effects. Portrait v2 supports portrait styling, while FLUX and Qwen are part of the local AI image pipeline that powers image-to-image transformations.

### Why is my hand not being recognized?

Lighting is the most common reason. Try facing a light source, keep your hand fully inside the camera frame, and avoid fast movements. A plain background also helps the gesture tracking stay accurate.

### The webcam view is blank. What should I check?

Close other apps that might be using the camera, such as video-calling programs. Then reopen GesturePortal. If the view is still blank, unplug and reconnect an external webcam, or restart your Windows computer.

### Can I use GesturePortal for anime-style visuals?

Yes. GesturePortal includes anime among its creative directions, along with other full-scene styling options. You can experiment with different looks and see how your gestures change the portal in real time.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Basics

If GesturePortal does not open, restart your computer and try again. Make sure the application finished downloading completely before opening it.

If gestures feel unreliable, improve the lighting and slow down. Keep your palm facing the camera and avoid partially hiding your hand behind your body or an object.

If styling feels slow, close other heavy programs running in the background. Local AI styling uses your computer's resources, so giving GesturePortal more room to work can help.

If the webcam disconnects, check the USB cable for external cameras. For built-in cameras, restarting the app usually restores the feed.

If Windows shows a camera permission prompt, allow access so the gesture-control features can see your hand.
